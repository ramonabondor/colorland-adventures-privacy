Colorland Adventures — Privacy Policy
Effective: 28 October 2026
Publisher: Bondor Ramona Anisoara
Privacy and support contact: bondorramona17@gmail.com

A game for children and their families
Colorland Adventures is a finger-painting game for children aged 3–7. It works offline. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases, or chat. It does not request internet, microphone, camera, location, contacts, storage, or advertising-ID permissions. We do not collect or transmit personal information through the app.

Purchase and payment
The game is planned as a one-time paid download, with an intended price of €2.99; the final price, taxes and currency can vary by region and the store. Google Play handles the purchase outside the game and may process account, payment and transaction information under Google's privacy policy. The publisher may receive sales and transaction records from Google Play for sales administration, support, accounting and any applicable legal obligations. The game itself does not request, receive or store payment details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Buying and downloading the game requires the store's online services; playing the installed game works offline.

What stays on your device
The app stores paintings as finger-stroke coordinates, selected paint colors and brush sizes. It also stores level numbers, completion milestones, stickers, tutorial status, language, sound and accessibility preferences, and saved-artwork timestamps and random painting identifiers. These identifiers distinguish local paintings; they are not advertising or device identifiers. The gallery contains the child's actual painting strokes. No names, photos, recordings, contact details, or precise location are requested.

Storage, retention and deletion
Paintings and preferences are stored in the app's private storage, without a server or cloud account. Android backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in the app configuration. Data remains until a parent deletes saved artwork, resets progress, clears app storage, or uninstalls the app. Reset Progress erases paintings, gallery versions, completion, stickers and tutorial progress; it retains language and audio/accessibility preferences. Individual gallery deletion does not erase the working painting or the level's earned sticker. Parent actions use a simple parent gate and deletion confirmation; this gate is a deterrent, not identity verification. There is no automatic expiry or cross-device sync. Device administrators and operating-system behavior are outside the publisher's control.

Sound and optional speech
Bundled sounds play locally. Optional spoken colors and instructions use Android TextToSpeech only when an installed voice is marked as available without a network connection and its language data is installed. Only preset game phrases are sent to that local speech service; the app does not record the child. If a suitable voice is unavailable, translated text remains and speech is disabled for that language. Installed speech services and Android are provided by the device vendor or another provider; their independent privacy terms may apply. Speech, sounds and music can be disabled separately.

Sharing, third parties and this website
The app sends no painting or progress data to the publisher or third parties and has no external sharing feature. Its parent area can open this public privacy website in a browser. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, contains no analytics, third-party fonts, scripts or forms, and does not receive app painting data. GitHub and your browser/network provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address, under their own policies. GitHub's privacy statement is linked below. Opening the site requires internet access in the browser, not in the game.

Contact and changes
Parents can contact bondorramona17@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. Please do not include a child's personal information or artwork containing personal information in an email. If you email us, your email provider and ours handle the message, and we use it to respond and address the request; you may ask us to delete that correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. We cannot retrieve paintings from a device. Material changes to app data behavior will require an updated notice and app release. This dated notice describes the implementation; it is not a certification of legal or store-policy compliance.
